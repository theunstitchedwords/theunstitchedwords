About Me

Words that perform.
Lines that linger.

Delhi · Available Worldwide
English · Hindi · Bengali · Assamese

I write as if memory were a country, and words are the only way back home. Originally from Guwahati, Assam, I grew up surrounded by landscapes that taught me how to notice, from the lushness, resistance, and silence, I shaped the way content could be structured and, more importantly, why.

Today, I work at the intersection of two worlds, performance and poetry.

As a content and copywriter, I create SEO-driven pieces that rank, landing pages structured for conversion, and copy designed to capture attention and drive action.

As a writer, I explore politics, memory, womanhood, and inheritance, with work published on The Hemlock Journal, BlahckSheep, and PoemsIndia, and recognised in literary spaces.

This duality is the edge I bring to the table. I understand how to connect people through search, through story, through silence. And I use that understanding to write words that are performative, and conversion-worthy.

If you’re looking for writing that is strategic without being hollow, and expressive without losing purpose, we’ll work well together.

The Storyteller

Literary &
Creative Writing

My work moves through human dogmatism of inheritance and language, through bartering prayers and a tongue that cannot always choose where it belongs.

So I learned to write with what remains: abstraction, hunger, and aftermath.

Published in The Hemlock Journal, BlahckSheep and PoemsIndia, recognised at the Delhi Poetry Slam, my writing exists where words are not just decoration, but witness. I don’t write to make gods out of my tongue. I write so that it does not drown.
